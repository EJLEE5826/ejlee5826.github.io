Selected Research

  • 2026
    HyCal: Cross-Discipline Few-Shot Class-Incremental Learning

    CVPR 2026 · Co-first author

    • Problem: prototype drift and degraded recognition across heterogeneous domains and imbalanced data
    • Approach: designed the XD-VSCIL benchmark spanning 8 heterogeneous domains and proposed a training-free prototype calibration that combines cosine similarity and Mahalanobis distance
    • Validation: evaluated under varying shot and imbalance settings, with per-domain, incremental-session, and calibration analyses
  • 2024
    Weakly Supervised Object Detection for Construction Sites

    IEEE Access 2024 · Co-first author

    • Problem: locating objects on construction sites accurately when only limited annotation is available
    • Approach: a weakly supervised framework built on an enhanced class activation map (CAM)
    • Validation: quantitative comparison against baselines on real construction-site images
  • 2025
    XTag-CLIP: Thyroid Scar Analysis with Limited Data

    AMAI @ MICCAI 2025 · First author

    • Fine-grained visual attribute recognition for medical images with limited data, using CLIP and cross-attention
  • 2026
    Cross-Dataset Continual Segmentation for Remote Sensing

    Ongoing research

    • Benchmark and evaluation of class-incremental semantic segmentation under sensor and annotation shift across remote-sensing datasets
