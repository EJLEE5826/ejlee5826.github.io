  2. CVPR
    HyCal: Few-Shot Continual Learning across Heterogeneous Domains
    IIPL · 2025.04–2026.01 · CVPR 2026 · Co-first author
    Role: Problem definition, experimental setup and benchmark design, implementation, validation and analysis, and writing.
    Few-shot class-incremental learning · Domain shift · Data imbalance
    • Designed the XD-VSCIL benchmark spanning 8 heterogeneous domains to evaluate settings where domain gaps and data imbalance occur together.
    • Implemented a training-free prototype calibration that combines cosine similarity and Mahalanobis distance to correct class representations without additional training.

  3. CIKMOral
    RI-Prune: Visual Token Efficiency for Multi-Image Vision-Language Models
    CIKM 2026 · Oral · Second author
    Visual token pruning · Multi-image understanding · Efficiency–performance trade-off
    • Analyzed the limits of attention-based token selection with multiple input images, and studied a selection method that needs neither additional training nor attention scores.
    • Scores tokens by query relevance, cross-image correspondence, and intra-image representativeness, and keeps a balanced share per image so each input’s information is preserved.
    • Evaluated the token-reduction vs. task-performance trade-off on several models and multi-image benchmarks, improving over existing pruning methods.

  4. WACVNeurIPS-W
    Background Shortcuts in Visual Recognition
    Learning to Detour (WACV 2024) · NegSep (NeurIPS 2026 VLM4RWD Workshop) · Second author on both
    Shortcut learning · Weakly supervised segmentation · Zero-shot OOD detection
    • Common theme: how a model’s reliance on object or background cues distorts its predictions, and task-specific ways to reduce it.
    • Learning to Detour (weakly supervised segmentation): an augmentation that separates and recombines object and background features to create diverse object–background pairs; analyzed background reliance and verified segmentation gains on PASCAL VOC and MS COCO.
    • NegSep (zero-shot OOD detection): separates the scores of background-related and unrelated negative labels, reducing the effect of background shortcuts on OOD decisions without additional training.
    Detour paper NegSep paper
